01 Health, Wellness & Longevity

Aging demographics + GLP-1 revolution + med spa fragmentation. Patient LTV grows 7x when you own the full patient journey.

How the Flywheel Spins

  • • Patient joins Gym ($40/mo) → notices aesthetics → visits Med Spa ($800 Botox)
  • • Med Spa NP flags fatigue → refers to Hormone Clinic → $300/mo TRT subscription
  • • Hormone blood work runs through your Lab (cost $15, bill insurance $150)
  • • Lab results → custom formulation at your Pharmacy → dispensed at cost
  • • PT treats gym injury → Billing Co captures 100% of insurance reimbursement
  • • Health Coach keeps patient engaged between visits → 3yr+ retention

Key Margins

  • • Med Spa: 40–60% EBITDA (cash-pay, no insurance)
  • • Hormone Clinic: 50–65% (subscription, low overhead)
  • • Diagnostic Lab: 35–45% (high fixed cost, high volume leverage)
  • • Compounding Pharmacy: 40–55% (503A = higher margin, 503B = scale)
  • • PT: 25–35% (insurance-dependent, volume play)
  • • Billing/RCM: 20–30% (recurring, sticky, low capex)

Why PE Loves This

  • • 10,000 Americans turn 65 every day — 20-year demographic freight train
  • • Healthcare is 18% of U.S. GDP with 80%+ fragmentation at the clinic level
  • • GLP-1 drugs opened a $100B obesity market — compounding pharmacies are the gateway
  • • Cross-referral turns $2K patient LTV into $15K+ — roll-up math is undeniable
  • • Med spa chains are the #1 healthcare PE target right now (L Catterton, TSG)

PE Platform Playbook: Health & Longevity Roll-Up

Platform: Acquire 3–5 location med spa group ($5–15M EBITDA) at 7–9x Add-Ons: PT clinics (4–6x), hormone clinics (5–7x), lab (6–8x), pharmacy (6–8x) Synergy: Cross-referral adds 30–50% same-store growth · Shared lab/pharmacy cuts COGS 15–20% Exit: Year 5–7 at 12–15x EBITDA to strategics (HCA, UnitedHealth Optum) or larger PE Target IRR: 25–35% · Equity Multiple: 3.0–4.5x
02 Construction & Real Estate Services

$600B+ home services market, 80% mom-and-pop. Vertical integration turns a 15% margin GC into a 25–30% margin platform.

How the Flywheel Spins

  • • Land Co options raw parcel for $500K → Permitting Co entitles it (6–12 mo) → value 3–5x
  • • Private Lending funds acquisition at 12% — interest is deductible to HoldCo, revenue to Lending Co
  • • GC builds using your Equipment (at cost) + Materials (at cost) → margin 25–30% vs 15% standalone
  • • Brokerage sells finished homes — 3% commission stays internal ($15K on $500K home)
  • • Property Mgmt runs retained assets — 8% of gross = $160K/yr on $2M property
  • • Home Services handles all maintenance calls — $89/call × 50 units/mo = $53K/yr recurring

Key Margins

  • • Land Acquisition: 20–50% ROI on entitled land flip (highest risk, highest return)
  • • GC: 15% standalone → 25–30% vertically integrated (equipment + materials internal)
  • • Equipment Leasing: 15–25% ROI on fleet (depreciation is tax shield)
  • • Private Lending: 10–14% yield + 2–4 pts origination (12–18% effective)
  • • Home Services (HVAC/Plumb/Elec): 20–35% EBITDA — the PE gold mine
  • • Property Management: 25–35% (low capex, high recurring)

Why PE Loves This

  • • Home services is a $600B market with 80%+ still mom-and-pop shops
  • • HVAC/plumbing is recession-proof: your furnace breaks, you pay — period
  • • Maintenance contracts = recurring revenue with 90%+ renewal rates
  • • Vertical integration adds 10–15% margin that standalone players cannot touch
  • • Equipment leasing adds a hard-asset collateral layer — lenders love it

PE Platform Playbook: Construction & Home Services Roll-Up

Platform: Acquire established GC ($10–20M rev) or regional HVAC/plumbing group ($5–15M EBITDA) at 6–8x Add-Ons: Equipment leasing co (4–5x asset value), materials yard, 8–15 HVAC/plumb/elec tuck-ins (4–6x each) Synergy: Internal equipment/materials cuts project costs 10–15% · Home services cross-sell to property mgmt Exit: Year 5–7 at 8–12x EBITDA to strategics (Lennar, ServiceTitan, larger PE) or IPO Target IRR: 20–30% · Equity Multiple: 2.5–3.5x
03 Technology / MSP & Cybersecurity

40,000+ MSPs in the U.S., most under $5M revenue. Platform + add-on model turns $2K/mo per client into $6K+/mo.

How the Flywheel Spins

  • • MSP signs 50-seat law firm at $175/seat = $8,750/mo base
  • • Cybersecurity Co does SOC 2 gap assessment = $15K project + $1,500/mo monitoring
  • • VoIP reseller ports their phones = $25/seat × 50 = $1,250/mo at 40% margin
  • • Backup/DR for their servers = $800/mo recurring
  • • Hardware VAR refreshes their endpoints = $60K one-time at 18% margin
  • • Client goes from $2K/mo (MSP only) to $6K+/mo (full stack) — 3× wallet share

Key Margins

  • • MSP: 35–45% EBITDA (labor-heavy but recurring, 90%+ retention)
  • • Cybersecurity: 40–55% (high-value consulting + recurring monitoring)
  • • VoIP/UCaaS: 30–50% (reseller model, low overhead)
  • • Hardware VAR: 15–25% (low margin but large deal sizes)
  • • Cloud Hosting: 40–60% (once infra is built, margin scales)
  • • Staffing: 15–25% placement fee (counter-cyclical balance)

Why PE Loves This

  • • 40,000+ MSPs — most sub-$5M, perfect roll-up math (buy at 6x, exit at 12x)
  • • Monthly recurring contracts with 90%+ retention — 24-month revenue visibility
  • • Cybersecurity is regulatory-mandated (SEC, HIPAA, state privacy laws) — forced demand
  • • Cross-sell adds 2–3× wallet share without new customer acquisition cost
  • • Thoma Bravo, Vista, KKR all actively building MSP/Cyber platforms

PE Platform Playbook: MSP & Cybersecurity Roll-Up

Platform: Acquire MSP with $3–8M revenue, 100+ managed clients at 6–8x EBITDA Add-Ons: Cybersecurity firm (6–8x), VoIP provider (5–7x), hardware VAR (4–6x), 5–10 smaller MSPs (4–6x) Synergy: Cross-sell security/VoIP/backup to every client · Shared NOC/SOC · Consolidated vendor purchasing Exit: Year 5–7 at 10–14x EBITDA to strategics (Cognizant, Accenture, CDW) or larger PE (Thoma Bravo, Vista) Target IRR: 25–40% · Equity Multiple: 3.0–5.0x
04 Logistics & Supply Chain

E-commerce created permanent demand. Reshoring means more domestic freight. Revenue per sq ft triples when you stack value-add services.

How the Flywheel Spins

  • • E-comm brand signs 3PL contract: 50K sq ft, 5K orders/day = $350K/mo base
  • • Your Trucking Co moves all inbound containers (port → warehouse) = $2,800/load × 20/mo
  • • Packaging Co does custom kitting for subscription boxes = +$40K/mo value-add
  • • Returns Co processes 1,500 returns/day (30% rate) = grading + liquidation rev share
  • • Last-Mile delivers big items = $85/delivery × 200/mo = $17K
  • • Fleet Maintenance services all trucks at cost — saves 30% vs dealer, cuts downtime

Key Margins

  • • 3PL Warehousing: 20–30% EBITDA (labor + real estate intensive)
  • • Trucking: 12–18% (fuel, driver pay, insurance — thin but essential link)
  • • Freight Brokerage: 15–20% gross (asset-light, tech-enabled — higher multiple)
  • • Packaging/Kitting: 25–40% (labor as primary cost, high value-add premium)
  • • Returns/Reverse Logistics: 30–45% (grading/refurb creates margin from "waste")
  • • Cold Chain: 25–35% (premium pricing, infrastructure barrier to entry)

Why PE Loves This

  • • E-commerce will hit $8T by 2027 — every dollar needs warehousing, transport, and returns
  • • Reshoring/nearshoring = more domestic freight — Mexico → U.S. corridors exploding
  • • 3PL is highly fragmented — 20,000+ operators, most single-facility. Clean roll-up math.
  • • Returns is the hidden gold mine — 20–30% of e-comm gets returned, growing 10%+ YoY
  • • Cold chain has natural moats (cap-ex, certifications) and 5–10 year contracts

PE Platform Playbook: Logistics & Supply Chain Roll-Up

Platform: Acquire regional 3PL ($10–20M rev, 200K+ sq ft) at 6–8x EBITDA Add-Ons: Trucking company (4–6x), last-mile provider, packaging/returns co (5–7x), cold storage facility Synergy: Internal freight keeps trucks 85%+ utilized · Returns is natural add-on to existing fulfillment clients Exit: Year 5–7 at 10–14x EBITDA to strategics (XPO, Ryder, DSV, Maersk) or infrastructure funds Target IRR: 20–30% · Equity Multiple: 2.5–3.5x
