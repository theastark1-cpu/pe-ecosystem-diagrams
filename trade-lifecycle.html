Trade Lifecycle Ecosystems

10 trade ecosystems where the school manufactures the workforce, the service company deploys it, and the supply chain captures every dollar of materials.

🔌 01 Electrical Trades Ecosystem

The school produces electricians. The supply house captures every panel, breaker, and foot of wire.

How The Flywheel Spins

Student pays tuition → graduates into Electrical Contracting Co at apprentice wage → journeyman buys materials from your Supply House at employee discount (still 20% margin to you) → upskills at your school into Solar/EV specialties at higher bill rates → retiree becomes Inspector signing off your own jobs. Lifetime revenue per electrician: $2-4M in wages + materials + tuition cycles.

🔥 02 HVAC & Climate Control Ecosystem

EPA-certified techs are the bottleneck. Own the certification pipeline. Own the equipment they install.

How The Flywheel Spins

Student earns EPA 608 at your school → hired by your HVAC Service Co → every install pulls equipment from your Distribution warehouse at wholesale → Ductwork Fab Shop supplies custom sheet metal → Energy Audit drives system replacement leads → Building Automation upsells smart controls to every commercial account. A single HVAC tech generates $500K+/yr in install revenue — you capture the labor margin, the equipment margin, and the ductwork margin.

🪠 03 Plumbing & Pipefitting Ecosystem

Every building needs water in and waste out. The school feeds the labor. The supply house captures every fixture.

How The Flywheel Spins

Student enters apprenticeship through your school → hired by Plumbing Contracting → every fitting and water heater comes from your Supply Wholesale → Water Treatment adds $3-8K to every service call → Drain Cleaning emergency calls feed plumbing repair leads → cross-cert in Gas Fitting at your school opens $120/hr+ commercial work. A plumbing company with captive supply chain runs 35-40% margin vs 20-25% standalone.

🛠️ 04 Welding & Metal Fabrication Ecosystem

AWS-certified welders are in permanent shortage. The school certifies them. The fab shop deploys them. The steel yard supplies them.

How The Flywheel Spins

Student earns AWS certs at your school → apprentices at your Fabrication Shop on real jobs → every stick of steel comes from your Metal Supply Yard at cost → top welders graduate into Pipeline Welding ($150K+/yr) or Structural Steel → Architectural Metal keeps the shop busy during slow industrial cycles with high-margin custom work. Steel at wholesale vs retail is a 30% margin spread on every job.

🚗 05 Automotive & Diesel Mechanic Ecosystem

Diesel techs start at $70K+. The school produces them. The parts store supplies every repair. The tow truck feeds the pipeline.

How The Flywheel Spins

Towing Co brings in broken vehicle → students diagnose/repair at school for real-world practice → your Parts Distribution supplies components at wholesale → car goes to your Used Car Lot → sold at retail margin. Meanwhile, your Fleet Maintenance contracts generate recurring B2B revenue with 90%+ retention. Every student who buys a tool or part from you stays in the ecosystem for 20+ years.

🪚 06 Carpentry & Millwork Ecosystem

Wood doesn't install itself. The school trains hands. The lumber yard supplies the material. The shop adds the margin.

How The Flywheel Spins

Student learns joinery at your school → hired by your Finish Carpentry Co → every board-foot of hardwood comes from your Lumber Yard at wholesale → top finishers graduate to Cabinet Shop ($50-100K kitchens) → Furniture Mfg turns shop offcuts into DTC products → Historic Restoration commands $150/hr+ with year-round backlog. Lumber at wholesale vs retail is a 25-35% margin spread on every job.

🏗️ 07 Heavy Equipment Operation Ecosystem

The machines cost $500K each. The operators cost $30/hr — unless you trained them yourself at $8K tuition.

How The Flywheel Spins

Student trains on your fleet at school → hired by your Excavation Co at apprentice rate → every machine comes from your Equipment Rental fleet — the same machines they trained on → Demolition feeds debris to your Trucking/Hauling → Land Clearing is the front-end for every construction project. The operator who learned on your machine buys fuel, maintenance, and eventually leases their own rig from you. Lifetime equipment revenue per operator: $200-500K.

🍳 08 Culinary & Hospitality Trades Ecosystem

Restaurants fail at 60% in year one. Your school feeds the kitchen. Your supply chain feeds the food cost. Your commissary de-risks the concept.

How The Flywheel Spins

Student trains in your school kitchen → stages at your Restaurant Group → every ingredient comes from your Food Supply/Distribution at 5-10% below market → school kitchen doubles as Catering Co commissary at night with student labor → Food Truck Commissary tests new concepts at low cap-ex before scaling to brick-and-mortar → Kitchen Equipment sells and services every oven and hood. A culinary student who stays in your ecosystem for 10 years generates $500K+ in combined food cost, equipment, and labor value.

🛩️ 09 Aviation Maintenance Ecosystem

50,000+ A&P mechanic shortage by 2030. The school certifies them. The MRO deploys them. The parts shelf stocks every repair.

How The Flywheel Spins

Student earns A&P at your Part 147 school → hired by your MRO / Repair Station → every part comes from your Aviation Parts Distribution at wholesale → top techs specialize in Avionics ($100-150/hr shop rate) → Detailing feeds pre-buy inspections into your MRO → your Aircraft Brokerage buys planes, your MRO does the pre-buy and repairs at cost, then sells at retail. An A&P generates $250-400K/yr in billable shop hours — you capture the labor, the parts margin, and the aircraft flip.

🚛 10 CDL / Commercial Driving Ecosystem

4 weeks from enrollment to licensed. The fastest talent pipeline in any trade. The truck sells the freight. The freight buys the fuel. The fuel runs through your card.

How The Flywheel Spins

Student pays $5-8K tuition → hired by your Trucking Co at training wage → your Freight Brokerage routes them premium lanes → your Repair Shop keeps the truck rolling → your Used Truck Dealership sells them a rig when they go owner-operator → your Driver Staffing places any overflow grads at a placement fee → they stay in your ecosystem buying fuel, brokered loads, and maintenance from you for 20+ years. A single CDL driver generates $250K+/yr in freight revenue. You capture the tuition, the freight margin, the fuel margin, and the truck sale.
