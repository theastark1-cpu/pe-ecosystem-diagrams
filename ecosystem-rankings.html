Key Takeaways

AI-Enhanced dominates both lists. The top 8 margin spots and top 8 multiple spots all belong to AI-augmented ecosystems. The reason is structural: SaaS software margins (60-80%) blend upward when paired with traditional service businesses (20-40%). A law firm alone might run 40% margin — but when its AI platform does 80% of the document review, the blended margin pushes past 50%.

Health & Longevity is the best non-AI play. At 40% blended margin and 13.5x exit multiple, the med spa/hormone clinic/lab/pharmacy circle is the highest-performing traditional roll-up. Cash-pay healthcare sidesteps insurance reimbursement headaches.

The bottom is hospitality and trucking. Restaurants, culinary trades, CDL/trucking, and high-touch hospitality all cluster at 12-22% margins and 5-8x multiples. These are volume plays, not margin plays. You need scale to make real money.

The sweet spot for starting: If you want high margins AND high exit multiples, pick any AI-Enhanced ecosystem (Legal AI, Accounting AI, Healthcare AI). If you want high margins but don't want to build tech, pick Health & Longevity or MSP/Cybersecurity. If you want recession-proof with decent margins and a talent moat, pick Electrical, HVAC, or Plumbing trades — 25-35% margins, 8-12x exits, and a school that prints your own workforce.
